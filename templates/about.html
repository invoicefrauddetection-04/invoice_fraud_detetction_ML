About ProcureGuard AI

ProcureGuard AI is an intelligent procurement invoice fraud detection system that combines Optical Character Recognition (OCR), Machine Learning, and Explainable AI to identify fraudulent invoices and support informed decision-making.

Objective

Detect fraudulent procurement invoices accurately while providing transparent AI-driven explanations.

Technologies

Flask, Python, OCR, Machine Learning, SHAP, Tableau, HTML, CSS, JavaScript.

Key Features

Invoice Processing, Fraud Detection, Explainable AI, Business Analytics and AI Investigation.

Workflow

Upload Invoice → OCR → AI Prediction → SHAP Explanation → Analytics & Reports.
